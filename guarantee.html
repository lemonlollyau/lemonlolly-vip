The guarantee

Miss the target. Get every cent back.

Last updated 20 November 2026

In short: before we start, we agree one growth target with you in writing. If we don't hit it within 90 days and you've done the things listed below, we refund every cent of the fees you paid us for those 90 days.

1. The target

Before any work starts, we agree one target with you and write it into your agreement. It is a single number we can both measure, such as qualified leads per month or monthly sales, with a starting baseline taken from your own figures.

2. The time frame

The target applies to the first 90 days, counted from your kickoff call.

3. Your side

The guarantee applies when you have:

  • given us access to the accounts we need within 7 days of kickoff
  • replied to new leads within the time we agree in your plan
  • kept the offer, pricing and capacity we planned around, or told us before changing them
  • joined a check-in call at least every two weeks
  • paid our fees on time

4. How to claim

Email indy@lemonlolly.com.au within 14 days after the 90 days end. We'll review the agreed numbers with you, using the same sources we used for the baseline.

5. What comes back

If the target wasn't met and your side was held up, we refund every cent of the fees you paid us for those 90 days, within 14 days of your claim, to the account you paid from.

6. What isn't covered

  • Ad spend paid directly to platforms such as Google or Meta. That money goes to the platform, not to us.
  • Third-party costs you pay directly, such as software subscriptions.
  • Work outside the plan we agreed.

7. Your agreement

These terms are part of your service agreement with lemonlolly. Your agreement records your target, baseline and plan.
