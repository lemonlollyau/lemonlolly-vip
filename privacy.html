Privacy

Your details, plainly.

Last updated 20 November 2026

What we collect

  • Your check-up answers and the score they produce.
  • The contact details you give us: name, email, business name and website.
  • Booking details when you schedule a call.
  • Basic visit data, such as which ad brought you here, so we can tell which ads work.

Why we collect it

To show your score, send your written diagnosis, prepare for and hold your call, follow up about it, and measure our advertising.

Who handles it for us

  • Web3Forms passes the check-up form to our inbox.
  • Calendly runs call bookings.
  • Google measures our ads, using cookies set by its ad tag.

We don't sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

Up to two years after your check-up, unless you become a client, in which case we keep it for as long as we work together and as the law requires after that.

Your choices

Email hello@lemonlolly.com.au to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to stop hearing from us.
