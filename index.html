Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

What is a growth clinic?

It's where businesses go when growth stalls. We don't start by selling you a package. We diagnose what's blocking your growth, prescribe a 90-day plan to treat it, and back it with a target we agree up front.

Is the check-up really free?

Yes. No card and no catch. You see your score the moment you finish. We only ask for your email if you want the written diagnosis or a call.

What happens on the diagnosis call?

It's a 30-minute video call. We walk through your written diagnosis, dig into your biggest limit and show you the plan we'd prescribe. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you, and you keep the diagnosis.

How does the guarantee work?

We agree one measurable target before we start. If we miss it within 90 days and you've done your part, every cent of our fee comes back. Ad spend paid to platforms like Google or Meta isn't our fee, so it isn't refunded. Read the full terms.

Where are you based?

Our team is in Adelaide, Australia, and has been helping businesses grow since 2014. Everything runs online, and diagnosis calls are booked in your afternoon, shown in your own time zone.

What if my problem isn't marketing?

Then we'll say so. If your business can't handle more customers yet, the check-up will show it, and we'll tell you to fix delivery before paying for a single extra lead.
